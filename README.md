# COUNTRY-DELIGHT-DAIRY-SALES-AND-INVENTORY-PERFORMANCE
**📌 Description**

The Country Delight Dairy Sales & Inventory Performance Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence dashboard designed to analyze and visualize sales trends, product performance, inventory health, and regional distribution for a dairy-focused organization.

It consolidates key performance indicators (KPIs) such as total revenue, average selling price, minimum stock thresholds, and quantity sold into a single, visually intuitive interface. The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor operational efficiency, understand customer demand, and make data-driven decisions related to pricing, inventory planning, and sales strategy.

🎯 **Purpose**

The primary purpose of this dashboard is to:

Provide a holistic view of sales and inventory performance across products, regions, and channels.

Support data-driven decision making for business managers and operations teams.

Identify top-performing and underperforming products.

Monitor inventory health to reduce stock-outs and minimize wastage.

Track monthly revenue trends to understand seasonality and growth patterns.

**🛠️ Uses**

This dashboard can be used for:

1. Sales Analysis

Compare revenue generated by different dairy products (Butter, Curd, Ghee, Lassi, Paneer, etc.).

Analyze the relationship between quantity sold and total revenue.

Identify the most profitable product categories.

2. Inventory Management

Monitor inventory health by product using color-coded indicators.

Ensure stock levels remain above minimum thresholds.

Support replenishment planning and reduce spoilage of perishable goods.

3. Channel Performance Evaluation

Analyze sales contribution from Online, Retail, and Wholesale channels.

Understand which channels drive the highest revenue.

4. Regional Insights

Visualize geographic distribution of sales across regions.

Identify high-demand and low-demand locations for targeted expansion.

5. Trend Monitoring

Track revenue trends over time (monthly).

Detect growth patterns, seasonal variations, and anomalies
